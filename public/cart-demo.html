Overview

Qonnect Billing is a revolutionary SaaS platform built specifically for WiFi service providers in Kenya. We understand the unique challenges of running an ISP business—from collecting payments via M-Pesa to managing MikroTik routers and tracking customer subscriptions.

With Qonnect Billing, you get a fully automated system that handles everything from customer sign-ups to payment processing, bandwidth management, and detailed analytics. Focus on growing your business while we handle the technical complexity.

🎯 Built for Kenya: Native M-Pesa integration, local support, and pricing designed for the East African market.

How It Works

Setting up your WiFi business with Qonnect Billing is incredibly simple. Here's the complete workflow:

  1. Create Your Account - Sign up in minutes with just your email and business details. No credit card required to start.
  2. Choose Your Package - Select a subscription plan that matches your customer base size and feature requirements.
  3. Configure M-Pesa - Add your M-Pesa Paybill or Till number. All customer payments go directly to YOUR account—we never touch your money.
  4. Connect MikroTik - Auto-configure your MikroTik router with our simple setup wizard. Supports Hotspot, PPPoE, and static IP configurations.
  5. Set Up Plans - Create your WiFi packages (daily, weekly, monthly) with custom pricing and bandwidth limits.
  6. Go Live - Share your customer portal link. Customers can purchase packages, and everything is automated from there!
⚡ Launch Time: Most ISPs go live within 30 minutes of signing up.

M-Pesa Integration

Our M-Pesa integration is the heart of Qonnect Billing. Here's what makes it special:

How Payment Flow Works

  1. Customer selects a WiFi package on your branded portal
  2. They receive M-Pesa STK push or use your paybill number
  3. Payment goes directly to YOUR M-Pesa business account
  4. Our system receives instant callback notification
  5. Customer account is activated within 5 seconds
  6. MikroTik router automatically grants network access

Setup Requirements

You'll need:

✓ M-Pesa Paybill or Till Number (we help you get one if needed)

✓ Safaricom API credentials (generated through Daraja Portal)

✓ Your business registration details

🔐 Security Note: Your M-Pesa credentials are encrypted and stored securely. We use bank-level security standards.

MikroTik Setup

Qonnect Billing works seamlessly with MikroTik routers using the RouterOS API. We support all major configurations:

Supported Configurations

Hotspot Mode

Perfect for cafes, hotels, and public WiFi. Users see a captive portal before access.

🔌

PPPoE Mode

Ideal for home internet services. Customers connect with username and password.

Static IP Mode

For business clients needing dedicated connections and fixed IP addresses.

Payment Terms

How Billing Works

Your subscription to Qonnect Billing is separate from your customer payments:

Your Subscription Fee: You pay us monthly for using the platform (KSh 2,999 - 12,999 depending on your plan). This is paid via M-Pesa to our paybill.

Customer Payments: Your customers pay YOU directly to YOUR M-Pesa paybill. We never touch this money—it's 100% yours.

Billing Cycle

Subscription fees are charged monthly on your sign-up date. For example, if you sign up on January 15th, you'll be billed on the 15th of each month.

Free Trial

All new accounts get a 14-day free trial with full access to all features. No credit card required. Cancel anytime during trial with no charges.

Refund Policy

If you're not satisfied within the first 7 days of your paid subscription, we offer a full refund—no questions asked.

📝 Note: Failed payments result in account suspension after 3 days grace period. Reactivation is instant upon payment.

API Documentation

For developers who want to integrate Qonnect Billing with their own systems, we provide a comprehensive REST API.

Getting Started with API

Available on Professional and Enterprise plans only. Generate your API keys from the dashboard under Settings → API Keys.

Base URL

https://api.qonnectbilling.co.ke/v1

Authentication

All API requests require Bearer token authentication:

curl -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY" \ https://api.qonnectbilling.co.ke/v1/customers

Common Endpoints

GET /customers - List all customers

POST /customers - Create new customer

GET /packages - List WiFi packages

POST /payments - Record payment manually

GET /sessions - List active sessions

GET /analytics - Retrieve analytics data

Webhooks

Receive real-time notifications for events like new payments, customer expiries, and network events. Configure webhook URLs in your dashboard.

📚 Full Documentation: Visit docs.qonnectbilling.co.ke for complete API reference with code examples in multiple languages.
